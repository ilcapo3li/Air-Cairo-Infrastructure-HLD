# 10. Assumptions
1. Application services are **stateless** and run on Linux, with a health-check endpoint each.
2. Application services use Azure SQL, Cosmos DB and Service Bus through standard SDKs.
3. The Azure subscription and the Cloudflare account are owned and paid by the client directly. We operate them with delegated access and do not resell cloud services.
4. Domain, DNS access and SSL ownership are provided by the client.
5. Mobile store accounts (Apple, Google) are owned by the client.
6. Setup scope is fixed to [Section 8](08-delivery-plan.md). Additional work is billed at on-call hourly rates.
7. Unused minimum hours do not roll over.
8. Prices are valid for 30 days.
9. The proposed architecture and sizing are an initial baseline. Features and sizes may be added or adjusted during hosting based on actual usage and monitoring results; changes affecting cost or scope are agreed with the client beforehand.
